Sweet spot — swing here for a six or four
How it works — six balls to an over, two overs, three wickets in hand. Each ball comes down the pitch at its own pace. Swing right on the bright band for a six, just off it for a four, further out for a single or two. Miss the timing badly and you're out; leave it alone for a dot ball.
